# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Circulo community a respectful
and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the community and project.
- Respect different experiences, opinions, and communication styles.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in public spaces or project activities.
- Trolling, insults, or deliberate disruption of discussions and reviews.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of
Conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate it. Maintainers may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report an incident privately through the reporting route in the
[Security Policy](SECURITY.md), or contact the maintainers through the project
website. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible.
Maintainers who receive a report must recuse themselves from enforcement if
they have a conflict of interest.
